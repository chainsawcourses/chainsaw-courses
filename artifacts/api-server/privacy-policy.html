1. Information We Collect

When you use Chainsaw Courses, we collect the following information:

  • Name and email address — provided when you activate your account
  • Course progress — your progress through training modules and quiz results
  • Device identifier — a unique ID stored on your device to prevent account sharing

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide access to your purchased training course
  • Track and save your progress through the course
  • Issue your certificate of completion
  • Respond to support requests

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except where required by law.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your information in transit and at rest.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at info@chainsawcourses.com.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@chainsawcourses.com.

7. Cookies

The app uses local storage on your device to maintain your session and save your device identifier. No third-party tracking cookies are used.
