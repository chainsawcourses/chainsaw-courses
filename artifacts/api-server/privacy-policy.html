1. Information We Collect

When you use Chainsaw Courses, we collect the following information:

  • Name and email address — provided when you activate your account
  • Course progress — your progress through training modules and quiz results
  • Device identifier — a unique ID stored on your device to prevent account sharing
  • Digital waiver signature — collected once on first use
  • Published app traffic and coarse operational events — approximate visitor, page, country, browser and device metrics, plus manual page load-failure and retry outcomes

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide access to your purchased training course
  • Track and save your progress through the course
  • Issue your certificate of completion
  • Respond to support requests
  • Operate and improve a reliable service by understanding approximate traffic and diagnosing whether manual pages load and recover successfully

Our lawful basis for the published traffic and operational analytics is legitimate interests: operating, diagnosing and improving a reliable service.

3. Data Sharing

Replit provides platform hosting and infrastructure. When analytics is enabled for the published app, Replit also processes IP traffic, network and browser data to provide approximate visitor numbers, top pages, countries, browsers and device types.

The app sends coarse operational event names when a manual page fails to load, a retry is attempted, or a retry succeeds. Event properties are limited to the reader surface, single-page or spread layout, initial or retry attempt, and request or image-decode failure type. Learner identity, manual page numbers, search text and manual content are not sent to analytics.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Analytics is not used for advertising or cross-site profiling.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your information in transit and at rest.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at info@chainsawcourses.com.

Replit retains analytics with the published project's history. The Publishing Analytics dashboard currently provides reporting periods of up to 30 days.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@chainsawcourses.com.

7. Cookies

The app uses local storage and essential cookies on your device to maintain your session and save your device identifier. We do not use advertising cookies. When published analytics is enabled, Replit adds its hosted analytics tracker as described above.
