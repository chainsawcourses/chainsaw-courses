Build a modern, interactive web application dashboard featuring a Biosecurity & Hazard Map tailored for UK chainsaw operators performing maintenance and cross-cutting tasks. 

Use HTML, CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript with the Leaflet.js library for a lightweight, interactive map interface. The UI must look like a professional safety dashboard, using a clean dark grid layout with high-visibility color-coding.

### 🗺️ Layout & Architecture
1. Left Sidebar: Control panel containing category headers, checkboxes to toggle map layers, and a dynamic "Active Hazard Information" display card.
2. Right Panel: A large Leaflet.js map initialized over a map of the United Kingdom, populated with colored mock polygons and circular coordinates representing risk zones.

### 📊 Categories & Layer Coding
The layers must be strictly divided into two primary structural categories, completely omitting any reference to stinging insects or specialized climbing suits:

#### Category 1: Direct Operator Health Hazards (Color Code: Vivid Orange/Red)
*   Oak Processionary Moth (OPM): White silken canopy nests. Running saws can aerosolize toxic urticating hairs, causing severe respiratory distress, skin blistering, and eye irritation.
    ↳ Required Controls: Visual canopy sweeps before cross-cutting oak logs; switch to sealed safety goggles and Respiratory Protective Equipment (RPE) if nests are present.
*   Brown Tail Moth (Euproctis chrysorrhoea): Infests scrub, hawthorn, and fruit trees. Toxic hairs cause severe dermal blistering and acute asthma tracking issues.
    ↳ Required Controls: Long-sleeved cut-resistant gear, face shields, and skin barriers during branch clearance.

#### Category 2: Statutory Containment Zones (Color Code: Vivid Purple/Blue)
*   Eight-Toothed Spruce Bark Beetle (Ips typographus): Regulated wood pest in Southeast England. Strict movement bans apply to raw conifer and spruce logs.
    ↳ Required Controls: Immediate pause on conifer cross-cutting operations; check timber movement licensing compliance before dragging or bucking stems.
*   Chalara Ash Dieback (Hymenoscyphus fraxineus): Extensively brittle timber profiles. Advanced decay causes ash crowns to unpredictably shatter or drop heavy limbs during ground tension processing.
    ↳ Required Controls: Establish double-distance safety zones around dead ash stems; minimize the use of heavy wedging or aggressive winching on brittle material.
*   Sudden Oak Death / Larch Dieback (Phytophthora ramorum): Highly transmissible fungal pathogen spread via contaminated wood shavings, sawdust, and bar oil.
    ↳ Required Controls: Complete tool, chain, guidebar, and boot washdowns using an approved chemical disinfectant before exiting the work site.
*   Sweet Chestnut Blight (Cryphonectria parasitica): Regulated fungal disease causing timber cankers.
    ↳ Required Controls: Processed sweet chestnut biomass must be incinerated on-site or subjected to deep burial; raw logs cannot be converted into transportable firewood.
*   Western Hemlock Dieback (Phytophthora pluvialis): Needle blight fungus tracking through wet soil.
    ↳ Required Controls: Mandatory cleaning loops for fuel jerry cans, support vehicle tires, and maintenance tools before relocating ground stations.

### ⚙️ Interactivity Logic
1. When a map layer checkbox is checked/unchecked in the sidebar, seamlessly toggle the corresponding colored zone indicators on the map.
2. Clicking a colored zone on the map (or selecting its profile header from an asset panel) must dynamically update the "Active Hazard Information" card in the sidebar.
3. The info card must cleanly display:
   - Common & Scientific Name of the Pest/Pathogen
   - Risk Classification (Direct Operator Threat vs. Statutory Containment)
   - Real-World Operational Impacts on Cross-Cutting & Maintenance
   - Mandatory Site-Specific Control Measures

Make the styling fully responsive for desktop screens and mobile tablets used out in the field, utilizing clean typography, generous padding, and visible layout rules. Ensure no third-party API keys are required by using standard open-source street maps.
