Dear IIRSM Team,

I am writing to enquire about the process for obtaining IIRSM CPD endorsement for a newly published chainsaw training course.

**About the Course**

*The Chainsaw Manual: Chainsaw Maintenance & Cross-Cutting* is an integrated theory-based learning programme combining a 138-page technical manual with a digital e-learning platform (chainsawcourses.com). The course is published by Overleaf Publishers Ltd and authored by David J Daniel.

Key programme details:

- **Scope:** Theory-only; covers occupational safety legislation, chainsaw mechanics, maintenance procedures, and cross-cutting techniques. Explicitly does not confer practical operational competency.
- **Guided Learning Hours (GLH):** 16 hours
- **Total Qualification Time (TQT):** 19 hours
- **CPD Points:** 5 verifiable CPD hours
- **Assessment:** 45-question randomised multiple-choice examination mapped to NPTC 0039-20 criteria; 80% pass threshold with auto-generated verifiable certificate
- **Target Audience:** Forestry workers, arborists, landscape professionals, and commercial chainsaw operators preparing for or supporting their NPTC practical assessment
- **Legislation Covered:** Health and Safety at Work Act 1974, PUWER 1998, COSHH 2002, MHOR 1992, PPE at Work Regulations 2022, CE/UKCA marking requirements

The course content has been internally audited against Bloom's Taxonomy and Ofqual learning outcome standards prior to this submission enquiry.

**Endorsement Interest**

We believe this programme aligns well with IIRSM's CPD framework and would benefit from formal endorsement to confirm its quality and credibility to forestry industry professionals across the UK.

1. The current endorsement application process and any associated fees
2. The documentation required for a CPD course submission (learning outcomes, assessment samples, author credentials, quality assurance evidence)
3. Your typical review timeline from submission to decision
