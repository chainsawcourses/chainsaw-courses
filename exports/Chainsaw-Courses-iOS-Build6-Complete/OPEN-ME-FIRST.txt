CHAINSAW COURSES — iOS 1.0.4 BUILD 6

This is the complete prepared Xcode project. Do not copy or replace individual files.

1. Open the App folder.
2. Double-click App.xcodeproj.
3. In Xcode, select the App target.
4. Under Signing & Capabilities, select Overleaf Publishers Ltd as the Team.
5. Confirm:
   Bundle Identifier: com.overleafpublishers.chainsawcourses
   Version: 1.0.4
   Build: 6
6. Select Any iOS Device (arm64).
7. Choose Product > Archive.
8. In Organizer, choose Distribute App > App Store Connect > Upload.

The duplicate PdfSaverPlugin.swift file is not included. The Xcode target compiles
the clean PdfSaverPluginFixed.swift file instead.
