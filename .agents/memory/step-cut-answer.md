## Rule
Module 43 Q1 correctOption = **1** ("When timber is under tension").

**Why:** PDF page 126 explicitly states: *"STEP CUT — This cut is really useful and great for severing small, medium and long pieces **under tension**."* The step cut keeps the chainsaw in the kerf by making the final cut on the tension side an inch behind the first cut, so the timber breaks off and the saw stays safely within the kerf.

The answer "When the bar is too short for the log" (option 2) is incorrect for this technique — oversized timber is handled by cutting from both sides or a bore cut, not a step cut.

**How to apply:** If this question is ever re-seeded or re-migrated, ensure correctOption = 1. A previous session incorrectly changed it to 2 and that change was reverted in a subsequent audit.
